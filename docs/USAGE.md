# Using BookMatch

Use the arrow beside **Your space** to collapse the sidebar into an icon rail. Hover over an icon to see its page name; use the arrow again to expand the menu. Page layouts adapt to the extra space.

- **Home:** your shelves, reading totals, and ten randomized picks from the top 75 popular books outside your library. Picks stay stable during the session, replace books you save, and reshuffle each time you open the app. Reading totals display three across in wide windows; narrower windows show On your shelves across the full width, with Reading now and Finished side by side below.
- **My Library:** add a book manually or find it in the 20,000-book offline catalog; search, sort, and filter by status or custom shelf. Use **Add a shelf** to create custom shelves, including empty ones. A book has one reading status and can belong to any number of custom shelves. **Choose a shelf** lets you select those shelves together; Add a book and Details offer the same choices. Click a shelf heading to collapse it to its name and count, or expand it to see its books. Home shows the same shelves, with its own remembered collapsed state. Large shelves have twelve books per page. **Manage shelves** can delete custom shelves without deleting their books, ratings, or notes. Want to Read, Currently Reading, Read, and Did Not Finish cannot be deleted.

  Saved ratings appear directly on book cards, including quarter stars. Open **Details** to edit metadata, dates, rating, notes, and review. Rating uses half- or full-star clicks, with optional quarter-star fine tuning. The title field is the same height as Author and Genre; long titles can be scrolled or read in a tooltip. Empty notes and review fields stay compact, expand when written in, and scroll for longer text.
- **Discover:** describe what you want and what you want to avoid. Filter all matching results by genre and publication year, with twelve books per page. New searches default to popularity based on Open Library reading logs; you can switch to best match, title, or publication year. Save or dismiss suggestions. BookMatch labels the matching method and explains the available evidence and gaps.
- **Stats & Goals:** view reading totals and set a dated book goal. Use **Edit goal** on a goal card here or on Home to change its name, date range, book target, and which books count. Choose **All books in My Library** for a general goal or **Only selected books → Choose books** for a custom goal. Search and check individual books, or filter to a shelf and use **Select all shown** to include its current books. Shelf shortcuts keep a fixed selection; books added to the shelf later are not added to the goal automatically. **Use selected count as target** sets a target of finishing the whole selection. You can also enter a different target. Saving updates the existing goal and recalculates progress from recorded finish dates. A selected book counts when marked Read with a finish date in the goal period. To remove a goal, open **Edit goal → Delete goal** and confirm; your books and reading history remain.
- **Catalog:** search 20,000 offline works by title, author, genre, or subject as you type; filter by genre, publication period, and whether a book is on your shelf; sort by popularity, title, or year. Click page numbers to move quickly, or an ellipsis to jump to any page. End arrows are disabled at the first and last pages. Saving books preserves your current catalog page and scroll position.
- **Settings:** preview and import a Goodreads CSV, import or export a BookMatch JSON backup, locate your database, install the optional local recommendation model, or clear saved books and reading history.

The optional model is `BAAI/bge-small-en-v1.5` through FastEmbed. Its one-time download is free and builds a local index of the bundled catalog. After installation, search text and your library stay on your computer. Without it, Discover uses a labeled metadata fallback. Catalog subject tags are incomplete and are **not** verified content warnings.

Book covers use the private cache in your local BookMatch data folder. Missing covers load from Open Library only when visible, using a public cover ID. If a cover is unavailable or you are offline, BookMatch shows its generated cover. Browsing a catalog book may send its cover ID to Open Library; it does not send your typed searches, notes, or Goodreads data. The cover collection is not bundled with the app or source repository; published app screenshots can display covers. A resumable, free [Archive.org cover download](../data/SOURCE.md) can populate the private cache for offline use.

In **Add a book**, **Find in offline catalog** searches locally and fills the selected work's available title, author, publication year, and a broad genre when its public subject tags support one. **Look up more details** is an optional, low-volume request to Open Library using only that public work ID. It can add a description. When opening **Details** for a catalog book, BookMatch also fills blank genre and description fields from local public metadata or looks up its description automatically if it has not been cached. It never replaces text you entered. Public values that still match Open Library are shaded and read only; missing fields and earlier personal edits remain editable. **Enter manually** in Add a book resets the catalog choice and unlocks the fields. Open Library records vary, so some fields may remain blank. Lookup responses are cached in your local data folder; typed search text and your Goodreads data are not sent.

## Clearing your library

Settings → **Clear saved books** removes every book from all shelves and clears dates, ratings, reviews, and notes after you type `CLEAR`. Shelf definitions, goals and their selected books, public metadata, downloaded covers and model remain. Goal progress returns to zero. Cancel leaves everything in place; a wrong confirmation shows an error. Existing backups remain separate. You may import a Goodreads export again after clearing, or add books again with fresh reading details.

## Backups

**Export library JSON** backs up saved books and personal reading history, shelves (including empty shelves), collapse preferences, and reading goals. Keep that file private. Export uses a temporary file and replaces the destination after writing succeeds. Imports preview records and report invalid entries; existing saved books and identical goals are skipped. Imports are limited to 20 MB per file.

## Theme

The current app has the light theme shown in the main README; there is no dark mode switch yet. The optional design-concept generator saves its output in the ignored `.local/dark-mode/` folder.
